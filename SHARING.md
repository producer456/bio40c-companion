# Course-material sharing

The project owner reported on September 20, 2026 that Douglas Woods approves sharing the supplied course information provided it is not sold. The 24 class documents in `sources/` are supplied under that reported permission for noncommercial study. Do not sell the materials. This is not a blanket open-source or commercial-use license.

Files are unchanged from the originals bundled in the companion app. Original authorship, image credits and notices remain in the documents, including materials crediting Jeff Schinske. Permission to share materials does not imply instructor endorsement of this student-built app. Consult the instructor before uses beyond the approved noncommercial sharing.

Some supplied documents are from earlier course editions and refer to older dates or other sections/instructors. Current announcements take priority; the companion's Course details feature can record corrections.

`sources/manifest.json` records the exact approved filenames, titles, sizes and SHA-256 hashes. No recordings, student submissions, personal coursework or native source history are included.

The OpenStax textbook is linked to its official site, not rehosted in full. The six existing textbook figures retain their separate CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 attribution in `figures/attribution.json`.
